    01 · Feeling

    Dread that is beautiful.

    Oppression is the baseline. Awe and epic power are punctuation, rare and expensive. Melancholy lives in the quiet moments between. Every page of the site keeps this balance.

    Visual
    Orange dusk against glass towers, black ink water, purple haze, sodium lights in fog.
    Sound
    Wind, siren silence, hum of machinery, hushed crowds.
    Feel
    Thin air, pressure, cold, weight in the limbs.

    02 · Colour

    Matter, not swatches.

    Every colour is a thing in the world. Two light conditions: Kayaba at dusk, the default, and the Trial at noon, the clean, empty city of the first dive. Contrast is measured live against the page.

      Power · Core

      Endro

      The heart of the power system. Energy and food for the Shardwell and anything connected to it.

      Chemical residue from reactions in the brain when feeling emotions. It floats around beings with emotions and physically exists. Inert in ordinary places; near the Godblood Zones it clusters into cell-like structures, visible as purple essence.

      • Core Any being with emotions produces it, by intensity. Each emotion gives a different flavor.
      • Core Spent Endro transforms into the effect: heat, motion, force. Nothing is lost.
      • Core Stable forever in storage.

      10 · Images

      Scale first, people small.

      Rules for every piece of concept art. Generated in the next phase, stored in the vault, linked to its note.

      • Scale. Kayaba is a continuous, layered mass. Five hundred floors; sixty is nothing. People are small against it.
      • Two lights. Kayaba at dusk (orange on glass, sodium in fog) or the Trial at noon (small white sun, perfect blue, clean concrete, blank white screens).
      • Water. Godblood is black ink with a sheen. Trial water is clear as glass, with the city still under it.
      • Endro. Purple haze, denser near the zones. Never sparks, never lightning.
      • Never. Neon, holograms, gadget sci-fi, friendly gods, homage to Dark Souls or Berserk.
